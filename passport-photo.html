    Frequently asked questions

    How do I make a passport size photo at home for free?

    Upload a clear front-facing photo to MergeMint's free Passport Photo Maker, drag and zoom so your face fits the frame, pick 35×45 mm or 2×2 inch, choose a background colour and download. You also get a printable sheet with up to 8 copies.

    What is the correct size for an Indian passport photo?

    Indian passport photos must be 35×45 mm. For a US visa, the size is 2×2 inches (51×51 mm). The tool offers both presets at exact 300 DPI print resolution.

    What background colour should my passport photo have?

    Plain white is standard for Indian passports. The tool also offers light blue and red backgrounds for documents that require them.

    How many copies fit on the print sheet?

    You choose — from 1 up to 8 copies in any columns × rows layout you like (for example 2×4). Photos are spaced with cutting gaps and the sheet auto-sizes to your grid.

    Will the photo print at the correct size?

    Yes. The photo is generated at 300 DPI (413×531 px for 35×45 mm) and the sheet notes its exact physical size. Just print at 100% scale with no fit-to-page scaling.
