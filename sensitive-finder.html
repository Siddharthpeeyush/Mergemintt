Sensitive Finder

Scan a PDF for Aadhaar numbers, PAN, mobile numbers and emails, then cover every match with one tap.

Drag & drop a PDF file here

or click to browse your device

    Aadhaar matches must also pass the Verhoeff checksum, so other 12-digit numbers are not flagged. The report shows masked values only — never full numbers.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does Sensitive Finder look for?

    Aadhaar numbers (checksum-verified), PAN numbers, Indian mobile numbers and email addresses in the PDF's text.

    Does it show my actual numbers?

    No. The report shows masked values only — for example 98•••10 — never full numbers.

    Is covering the same as deleting?

    Covering hides the text visually on the page. For a copy that must be unrecoverable, share a flattened image copy (PDF to JPG) instead.
