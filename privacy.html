Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

Your files never leave your device. Every MergeMint tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We don't upload, store, see, or share your documents or images — there is no server receiving them in the first place.

What we collect

  • Your files: nothing. Files you open in our tools are processed locally in your browser tab and never sent anywhere.
  • Accounts: none. There is no sign-up, no login, and no personal data to manage.
  • Analytics: we may use privacy-friendly, aggregate visit statistics to understand which tools are popular. These never include your file contents.

Cookies and advertising

We plan to show ads to keep MergeMint free. Our advertising partner (Google AdSense) may use cookies to show relevant ads and measure their performance. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Learn more in Google's advertising privacy policy.

Third-party libraries

Our tools load open-source JavaScript libraries (such as pdf-lib, pdf.js, and JSZip) from public CDNs. These libraries run locally in your browser; loading them does not send your files to anyone.

Children

MergeMint is a general-purpose utility site not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the contact details on our home page.
